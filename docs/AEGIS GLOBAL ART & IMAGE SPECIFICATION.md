# AEGIS GLOBAL ART & IMAGE SPECIFICATION
## Global Visual Language, Asset Design & Image Generation Standard

**Specification ID:** AEGIS-ART-IMAGE-GLOBAL  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** Global AEGIS Visual Ecosystem  
**Status:** Foundational Visual Specification

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The AEGIS Global Art & Image Specification defines the common visual language governing AEGIS imagery, three-dimensional assets, interface objects, architectural elements, technical artifacts, and visual representations.

The objective is not to make every AEGIS object look identical.

The objective is to make every object appear to originate from the **same technological civilization**.

An unfamiliar AEGIS artifact will remain recognizable as AEGIS before its function is known.

Every object expresses both:

1. **AEGIS Identity**
2. **Object Function**

The visual system therefore follows the principle:

> **Shared Origin. Distinct Function.**

---

# 2. GLOBAL DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

## 2.1 Function Shapes Form

An object's purpose influences its physical appearance.

Function is communicated through:

- silhouette
- proportions
- articulation
- mechanical structure
- sensor configuration
- containment geometry
- tools
- docking mechanisms
- energy routing
- protective structures
- movement architecture
- optical systems
- crystal geometry
- illumination

A Builder looks capable of building.

A Scout looks capable of observing.

An Umbilical looks capable of reaching and establishing connection.

A Portal looks capable of providing passage.

A Reagent looks capable of being inserted, combined, and transformed.

Function is visible before explanation.

# 3. AEGIS VISUAL DNA

All AEGIS technological artifacts inherit a recognizable family of characteristics.

## Primary Characteristics

- polished metallic construction
- precision mechanical engineering
- smooth engineered surfaces
- dark mechanical substructure
- crystalline components
- transparent energy channels
- visible internal illumination
- iridescent optical behavior
- modular construction
- deliberate articulation
- functional geometry

These characteristics form the visual genetic language of AEGIS.

Individual object classes may emphasize or suppress particular characteristics while retaining enough shared features to preserve lineage.

---

# 4. AESTHETIC CHARACTER

The global AEGIS aesthetic is:

**Advanced Mechanical + Crystalline + Iridescent + Luminous + Precision Engineered**

It may contain subtle organic flow in its geometry, but its construction remains unmistakably technological.

AEGIS technology may appear extraordinarily advanced.

It does not appear crude.

It does not appear randomly assembled.

It does not appear conventionally industrial.

It appears **engineered with extraordinary precision**.

# 8. IRIDESCENCE

Iridescence is a defining characteristic of AEGIS imagery.

It appears primarily in:

- crystal
- glass
- transparent energy chambers
- energized surfaces
- optical sensors
- portals
- active conduits

Iridescence may shift between:

**cyan → blue → violet → magenta → amber → gold → emerald**

The effect remains luminous and crystalline rather than oily or pearlescent.

Iridescence represents complex internal energy interacting with structure.

---

# 9. LIGHTING LANGUAGE

AEGIS technology appears alive with internal activity.

Illumination originates primarily **inside the artifact**.

Typical lighting sources include:

- crystal cores
- energy conduits
- optical sensors
- power nodes
- docking interfaces
- containment chambers
- thin mechanical seams
- portal surfaces

External lighting supports the form.

Internal lighting reveals the object's state.

---

# 10. ENERGY CONDUITS

Energy pathways may appear through transparent or translucent structures.

They may resemble:

- luminous filaments
- flowing plasma
- streams of colored light
- energized glass tubing
- crystalline channels
- contained spectral energy

Energy movement follows functional pathways.

Random decorative glowing lines are avoided.

---

# 11. MECHANICAL DETAIL

Mechanical complexity communicates capability.

- articulated joints
- layered rings
- pistons
- rotating collars
- magnetic contacts
- sensor arrays
- modular ports
- precision clamps
- cable channels
- tool mounts
- mechanical hinges
- containment ribs

---

# 12. SMOOTHNESS STANDARD

AEGIS surfaces favor:

- polished
- smooth
- machined
- streamlined
- controlled curvature
- precision segmentation

Moderate mechanical complexity is encouraged.

Excessive surface noise is avoided.

### Avoid

- random greebling
- excessive bolts
- unnecessary exposed wires
- rough cast-metal appearance
- junkyard construction
- extreme battle damage
- corroded machinery

AEGIS technology looks maintained and intentional.

---

# 13. CONTROLLED ORGANIC INFLUENCE

AEGIS technology may contain organic qualities in:

- curvature
- branching
- articulation
- movement
- growth-like structural relationships
- flowing energy paths

However:

> **Organic influence affects geometry, not material identity.**

An Umbilical may move like a tendril.

It remains unmistakably mechanical.

A structure may branch like a living organism.

It remains precision engineered.

Avoid forms resembling:

- vegetables
- flesh
- bone
- roots
- biological organs
- insects unless explicitly required
- uncontrolled biomorphic growth

# 25. 3D CONVERSION STANDARD

Images intended for conversion into 3D assets use:

- complete unobstructed silhouettes
- clearly separated appendages
- readable joints
- visible major mechanical structures
- minimal overlapping geometry
- neutral positioning
- predictable perspective
- sufficient spacing between objects

Objects intended for rigging use clear articulation points.

Examples:

- shoulder pivots
- elbow joints
- wrist interfaces
- finger joints
- sensor pivots
- ring rotation axes
- telescoping segments
- docking articulation

---

# 26. NEUTRAL ASSET POSE

Reference images intended for 3D conversion depict the object:

- stationary
- fully visible
- not interacting with another object
- not performing its task
- not holding unnecessary props
- not obscured by effects

Function is communicated by **equipment**, not action poses.

# 28. IMAGE COMPOSITION FOR ASSET SHEETS

For four objects:

**2 × 2 grid**

For eight compact objects:

**4 × 2 grid**

Each object receives:

- adequate negative space
- similar visual scale
- unobstructed silhouette
- consistent camera treatment

Objects do not overlap.

---

# 29. CAMERA STANDARD

Preferred reference views:

- front three-quarter
- straight front
- side where mechanically useful

Three-quarter views reveal:

- depth
- joints
- tools
- containment structures
- mechanical layering

Extreme perspective distortion is avoided for 3D reference imagery.

# 30. DETAIL HIERARCHY

Detail follows three levels.

## Level 1 — Silhouette

Identifies object class.

## Level 2 — Major Structure

Communicates function.

## Level 3 — Surface Detail

Communicates technological sophistication.

Level 3 never overwhelms Levels 1 and 2.

# 31. VISUAL STATE LANGUAGE

AEGIS objects may visually express operational state.

### Dormant
Minimal illumination.

### Available
Low steady internal illumination.

### Seeking
Directional sensor or docking illumination.

### Connected
Connection points illuminate.

### Active
Energy pathways become visible.

### High Activity
Increased internal luminosity and energy movement.

State is communicated through energy behavior rather than drastic physical redesign.

# 33. IMAGE GENERATION MASTER PROMPT

Use the following foundation when generating new AEGIS production assets:

**Create a high-detail 3D AEGIS technological artifact representing {OBJECT TYPE} with the function {FUNCTION}.**

Use polished chrome and silver armor, dark gunmetal mechanical substructure, brushed titanium precision components, smooth engineered surfaces, articulated mechanical construction, faceted translucent iridescent crystals, transparent energy conduits, and controlled internal illumination.

Use the AEGIS luminous spectrum of electric cyan, blue, violet, magenta, amber, gold, emerald, and teal.

The object's silhouette and equipment visibly communicate its function.

Maintain an advanced mechanical/crystalline technological aesthetic with controlled flowing geometry while avoiding biological appearance.

Use precision-machined construction, functional mechanical detail, modular interfaces, and purposeful articulation.

For production assets:

- neutral idle configuration
- no action being performed
- full object visible
- clean silhouette
- transparent background
- no environmental scenery
- no text
- no labels
- no unnecessary props
- suitable for conversion into a 3D GLB asset
- suitable for rigging when articulated

The artifact belongs unmistakably to the same technological civilization as all other AEGIS objects while retaining a unique function-specific identity.
