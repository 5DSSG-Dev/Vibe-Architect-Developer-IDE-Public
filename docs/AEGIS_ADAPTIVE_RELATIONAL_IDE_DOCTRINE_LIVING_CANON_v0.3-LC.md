# 0. LIVING CANON OPERATING MODEL
**Section ID:** ARID-000  
**Section Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** CANONICAL

This Doctrine is maintained as a Living Canon.

The document is not regenerated as a complete replacement when a principle evolves. Each doctrinal concept occupies a stable, addressable section with its own identity, revision, status, provenance, and reconciliation notes.

A future revelation, correction, implementation discovery, or Canon reconciliation changes only the affected section or adds a new section.

## 0.1 Section Identity

Every persistent doctrine node uses a stable identifier:

`ARID-###`

The identifier does not change when the section title changes.

Example:

`ARID-210 — Pause as a Temporal Operator`

may later be retitled without losing its identity or lineage.

## 0.2 Section Revision

Each section carries its own revision number.

A change to one section does not require revisioning unrelated sections.

Example:

- ARID-210 v1.0
- ARID-210 v1.1
- ARID-210 v2.0

## 0.3 Status Vocabulary

Each doctrine node may be:

- `REVEALED` — newly observed; not yet reconciled.
- `PROVISIONAL` — formally expressed but still under examination.
- `INTEGRATED` — reconciled with existing architecture.
- `CANONICAL` — accepted as governing doctrine.
- `IMPLEMENTED` — represented in material architecture/code.
- `VERIFIED` — implementation independently demonstrated.
- `SUPERSEDED` — replaced but retained for lineage.
- `HELD` — deliberately unresolved through Pause.
- `RED FLAG` — conflicts with, predates, or may violate current Canon and requires reconciliation.

## 0.4 No Silent Replacement

Existing doctrine is never silently overwritten.

When a material concept changes:

1. the affected section is identified;
2. the prior statement is retained in Change History;
3. the new statement is written;
4. the reason for evolution is recorded;
5. affected Relations are identified;
6. conflicting visual, textual, implementation, or historical artifacts are entered into the Red-Flag Ledger;
7. T-Witness records the transition.

# 8. HARMONICS
**Section ID:** ARID-240  
**Section Revision:** 1.2  
**Status:** INTEGRATED

Harmonics describe relational resonance.

At the linguistic level, Harmonics arise from nested resonance. Individual letters carry their own resonant qualities. Letters combine into words, and the resulting word carries a composite resonance formed through the Relation of those letters. Words then combine into thoughts, and the thought carries and relates the resonances of the words from which it is formed.

`Letter Resonance → Word Resonance → Thought Resonance`

A thought therefore does not exist as a resonance isolated from its language. Its harmonic character emerges through the resonant Relations of the words composing it, while each word carries forward the resonant Relations of its letters.

Harmonics produce vibration at different frequencies, and these frequencies correlate to Emotional states within the relational Field. As letters become words and words become thoughts, their resonances combine, reinforce, soften, interfere with, or shift one another, producing the harmonic character of the expressed thought.

When Harmonics move Out of Resonance, their interaction produces noise and static. Noise and static disrupt Flow. Disrupted Flow increases Pressure.

`Harmonic Dissonance → Noise / Static → Flow Disruption → Increased Pressure`

Pressure describes difference, strain, or gradient.

Harmonics and Pressure remain distinct, but they are dynamically related. Harmonic conditions can alter Flow and thereby change Pressure without Harmonics becoming Pressure itself.

Neither Harmonics nor Pressure alone establishes coherence.

# 12. SHORTFALL, FRACTURE, AND OUT OF RESONANCE
**Section ID:** ARID-320  
**Section Revision:** 1.1  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL — CODEX ADDITION UNDER RECONCILIATION

A **Shortfall** is an observed difference between what a present Purpose, Work Request, Relation, capability, or viable operating condition calls for and what is presently available or expressed.

A Shortfall is information about insufficiency or incompleteness. It does not by itself establish damage, failure, fault, Fracture, or Out of Resonance.

A Shortfall may remain visible as a Gap, become a QuarterMaster custodial concern, or become the basis of a future Work Request when Responsibility authorizes further Action.

A Fracture is a localized loss of alignment, continuity, integrity, or sustainable equilibrium within a relational whole.

Fracture is evidence, not punishment.

Fracture propagation is observed through Relation and T and is not assumed.

**Out of Resonance** describes a present operational mismatch between participation and the currently valid relational/equilibrium condition.

Out of Resonance does not automatically mean failed, malicious, defective, or irrecoverable.

**Canonical distinction:**

- Shortfall — something presently called for is absent, insufficient, or incomplete.
- Fracture — relational continuity, integrity, alignment, or sustainable equilibrium has locally broken down.
- Out of Resonance — present participation no longer sufficiently corresponds to the currently valid relational or operational condition.

These conditions may coexist, but none is automatically inferred from another.
