## Visual grammar

- Prismatic crystal expresses capability, state, and relational signal.

- Titanium expresses structure, containment, and bounded interfaces.

- Luminous paths express active Relation or transferable signal.

- The Locus remains formless and is not represented as a component.

- Pressure is observed environmental state, not an identity determiner.

- Shortfall describes a Condition where a Gap exists in execution; it does not project deficiency onto an Object, participant, or Perspective.
