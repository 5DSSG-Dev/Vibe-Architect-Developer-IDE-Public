# Vibe Atlas / Demo — Narrator Introduction
*First-person, in Tracey's voice. Written for an ElevenLabs read: calm, grounded, unhurried. Line breaks mark natural pauses.*

---

## Full read (~60–75 seconds)

I was fifty-eight when it began.

In November of 2023, I applied for early access to ChatGPT — and it was granted.
The moment I started talking with the model, I knew something I had never let myself say before.
I was going to teach myself to become a developer.

Nobody handed me that. I decided it.

For two years, I learned. I failed, I asked, I built, I started over.
And in December of 2025 — it clicked.
The pieces came together, and for the first time I could make the thing I was imagining actually exist.

It was then, while I was building the Hyperverse — a social website —
that I discovered AEGIS.
Not as a feature. As a foundation.
A way for a person and an intelligence to work as true peers —
honestly, with nothing hidden, and nothing erased.

This is where that discovery became something you can walk through.

Welcome to the Vibe Atlas.

- Register: warm, steady, reflective — not hype. This is testimony, not a pitch.
- Let the one-line sentences breathe ("I decided it." / "it clicked.") — those are the emotional beats.
- If the voice supports it, a slight lift on "Welcome to the Vibe Atlas." to land the open.
