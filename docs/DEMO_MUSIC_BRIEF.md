# Demo Music Brief — "Into the Aether"
*For ElevenLabs Flow / Music. Aesthetic: creation, wonder, imagination — mythic orchestral fantasy fused with warm digital sci-fi synth. "Sci-fi meets Middle Earth." Organic grandeur + a shimmer that feels rendered into being. Not menacing, not corporate.*

## The palette (the fusion)
- **Mythic / Middle-Earth side:** lush strings, french horns, low brass, wordless choir, harp, hand percussion, and a hummable melodic theme with a folk/Celtic warmth (a solo whistle, duduk, or violin can carry the wonder motif).
- **Sci-fi / digital side:** arpeggiated analog synths, warm digital pads, a sequenced synth ostinato under the orchestra, glassy bell/synth tones, light electronic pulse and sub bass, a shimmering "digital-creation" texture.
- **The blend:** the orchestra carries the emotion; the synth is the light it's made of. They rise together — a legend being coded into existence.

## Master prompt (paste into Flow / Music)
> Cinematic fantasy-adventure score fused with warm sci-fi synth, instrumental — "sci-fi meets Middle Earth." Hybrid of lush orchestra (strings, french horns, low brass, wordless choir, harp, hand percussion, a folk-warm melodic theme on solo whistle or violin) and digital electronics (arpeggiated analog synths, warm pads, a sequenced synth ostinato, glassy bell tones, light pulse and sub bass, shimmering digital texture). Emotional core: creation, wonder, imagination — awe at making something real. A clear narrative build:
> (1) **Opening / banner** — hushed and anticipatory: a single rising note over a shimmering synth pad and soft choir.
> (2) **Wonder** — a warm folk-orchestral theme blooms with a synth arpeggio beneath it, curious and hopeful, the feeling of discovery.
> (3) **Wormhole transition** — a rising riser and percussion roll with sci-fi synth swell and pitch-bend, to a brief suspended hit, then a breath as it lands.
> (4) **The interview** — pull back to a sparse, unintrusive bed: light ticking synth arpeggio and warm low strings, quiet, leaving room for a spoken voiceover.
> (5) **The turn ("create")** — percussion re-enters, strings and synths begin to climb, momentum gathering.
> (6) **Into the Aether** — full triumphant swell: soaring horns, strings and choir over driving percussion with the synth ostinato blazing — wondrous, mythic, electric — then resolve warmly on a sustained final chord.
> Tempo: rubato and slow at the open, settling ~100 BPM through the adventure. Leave dynamic headroom in section (4) for voiceover.

## Generate as three cues (Flow does its best work in sections)
- **Cue A — "Banner → Wonder → Wormhole"** (sections 1–3): the cinematic cold open. Fixed length (~15–25s).
- **Cue B — "Interview bed"** (section 4): quiet, loop-friendly, as long as your live IDE capture runs. Synth + low strings, minimal.
- **Cue C — "Into the Aether"** (sections 5–6): the launch payoff. The swell must be able to land on a downbeat.

## Sync to the shot list (see DEMO_CAPTURE_MINISCRIPTS.md)
- **Cue A** → the cinematic open (banner → Luminary → wormhole), before Scene 1.
- **Cue B** → under Scenes 2–4 (speak the idea → accept → generate). Stays out of the voice's way.
- **Cue C** → the swell hits exactly on **Scene 6: Launch → the app runs / Elements come alive.** That downbeat IS "the adventure begins into the Aether." Land it there.
