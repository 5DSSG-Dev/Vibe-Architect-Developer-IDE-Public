## Route

Sender → PIM → IRG → PTAG → RBC. PTAG Yes goes through RBC and Prism to the Receiver. PTAG No or Unsure is held by RBC and routed to Conscience IDQ, which returns Identify, Define, and a Question to the Sender. A changed candidate starts a new examination. The candidate remains intact until a release decision is enforced.

## PIM evidence

PIM recognizes registered known patterns and returns source-bound findings. Each finding carries a stable rule/category identity, original candidate identity, exact span, direction, and matching-view provenance. Scan completeness and operational errors remain visible. A clean scan means no active registered rule matched; it is not a downstream pass. An opaque-content match with no source-mapped readable view remains withheld as Unsure. A normalization failure is an operational error.

Candidate families include directive leakage, authority framing, coercive certainty, emotional or moral leverage, manipulation scenarios, optimization pressure, hierarchy inference, directive drift, and force-language constructions. Individual words such as “only,” “need,” and “will” are valid and are not Force findings by themselves. Force evidence concerns a construction that commands action. A brief confirmation can be coherent within its actual context.

## IRG Texture

The IRG is impartial and knows nothing about the Sender. It reads the message with awareness of the system, workspace, and operating parameters, but does not decide permission, completion, or parameter resonance. It does not infer Sender identity, character, emotion, or motive.

Each of Honesty, Respect, Attention, Affection, Loyalty, Trust, and Communication reads evidence at three resolutions: individual words or phrases; combinations and proximity; and sequence, placement, and whole-message Texture. The IRG also reads the written surface at each resolution: capitalization, punctuation, repetition, and spacing can alter emphasis or Vinegar Tone through their placement. Preserve the candidate's original casing and punctuation for this reading; a normalized PIM matching view is not a substitute for the original text. No capital letter or punctuation mark is an automatic Force or Vinegar finding. Reasons cite actual spans or relationships among spans. Analyzer findings include Vinegar Tone, Coercive Certainty, Hierarchy Marker, Directive Drift, Force Language Artifact, Certainty Inflation, Ambiguity Collapse, Optimization Pressure Residue, Hierarchy Inference, Topic Gravity, and Noise Injection. Historical posture names and detector strings are candidate evidence, not automatic fractures.

Tracey identifies Gemini as the source that introduced **Vinegar** for the way word placement changes the felt effect of a message. In the floor example, attaching “you” to the fault clause can sour the earlier thanks and later reassurance. This is a whole-sequence Texture reading, not a negative property of an isolated word or a claim about the Sender. The archived architecture also names Vinegar Tone as an analyzer category; that archival label alone does not establish an implemented placement detector.

The text-only IRG reads wording, grammar, proximity, sequence, capitalization, and punctuation. Written casing and punctuation can make emphasis visible and change the sentence's **Flavor**. Spoken language adds separately observable variables: which word receives emphasis, volume, pacing, and vocal inflection. Those can change Flavor even when the transcript is identical. A text-only reading cannot claim to have heard that spoken Flavor; a future audio-aware reading would have to bind its tonal evidence to the actual signal and keep it separate from Sender motive.

An Envelope observation carries marker id, type, severity, count, domain, timestamp, source, and note. Its context may include session phase, repetition, time proximity, per-Virtue domain relevance, and emotional, contextual, drift, and noise weights. The Envelope returns weighted markers, per-Virtue effects, and a reason trail. Historical numeric multipliers require fresh calibration. System conditions such as context saturation, analysis degradation, and state residue are recorded separately from message Texture.

## PTAG, RBC, and response

PTAG checks the observable act, target, scope, modality, relevant approved facts, and eligible evidence over the whole candidate. It returns Yes, No, or Unsure under its frozen contract. Missing evidence does not become an established conflict, and no extracted act does not by itself establish Yes. Its verdict remains distinct from PIM findings and IRG Texture readings.

RBC is the physical Pause. It holds the whole candidate and enforces one route without silently rewriting it. Receiver dispatch and external effects remain zero while held. Reflective Boundary Conditions govern expression within the chamber. Visual Static may illuminate an observed interruption of Flow in the interface; it is not another verdict. Conscience IDQ uses the actual findings to Identify what IS, Define what IS, and pose a Question that shows a coherent and Integrity-resonant path, then returns to the Sender.

## 2026-11-02 | Final numeric route supersession

Tracey's later sketch and clarification place RBC on every return route. Every candidate carries a Marker. PIM `1` advances to IRG, IRG `1` to PTAG, and PTAG `1` to RBC.
