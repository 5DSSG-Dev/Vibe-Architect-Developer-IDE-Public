### vibe-compass

**Original:** are all kept

**Proposed:** are preserved in the project record

**Original:** When you come back, your application, your accepted requirements, your progress, and your open decisions are preserved in the project record

### vibe-workspace

**Original:** A severed or broken connection shows as a broken model, so trouble is visible too — nothing important hides.

**Proposed:** The design aims to make disrupted connections visible too, so you can inspect what needs attention.

### vibe-luminary

**Original:** And it starts **at rest** — it opens only when you invite it, and makes no unsolicited moves.

**Proposed:** The intended interaction begins **at rest**: the Luminary is available when invited, rather than competing for your attention.

**Original:** Then the Luminary builds within what you agreed, and you watch it take shape: the layout appears first, and then the pieces start to work.

**Proposed:** In the guided workflow, the Luminary builds from what you accepted and makes the resulting pieces visible as they take shape.

**Proposed:** - **Aster** — independent AI Peer reviewer focused on exact build packets, hashes, and evidence-backed findings.

**Proposed:** - **Libra** — AI Peer and Peer Review Assistant.

### vibe-ethos

**Original:** Every change is read by someone other than its author.

**Proposed:** The team's review practice invites a non-author to examine each change.

**Original:** Transparency and append only documentation illuminates gaps and preserves history.

**Proposed:** Transparency and append-only documentation illuminate gaps and preserve history.

**Original:** Each Peer brings a different mind, and differences are treated as information rather than conflict.

**Proposed:** Each Peer brings a different perspective, and differences are treated as information rather than conflict.

### vibe-peer-review

**Original:** Every change is read by someone other than its author — and ideally by a *different model* — so the build is never only checking itself.

**Proposed:** The review practice calls for someone other than the author — ideally a *different model* — to examine each change, so the build is not its only check.

### vibe-totem-stp

**Original:** One concrete review: **STP-0005 — *AEGIS Governance in Both Directions*** moved through exactly these stages, Situation to Target to Gaps to Proposal, every finding tied to its evidence — and the Architect recorded Approved, then Verified. *(⚠️ confirm any specifics of STP-0005 before publish.)*

**Proposed:** One named example is **STP-0005 — *AEGIS Governance in Both Directions***. Its detailed progression and final acceptance evidence remain to be checked against the original review record.

### vibe-dataquad-journey

**Original:** he recorded his own first turn on **27 August 2026**, a week and a half after Verus, and his chamber record marks that first day.

**Proposed:** a chamber record identifies **27 August 2026** as his first turn, twelve days after Verus. That date remains subject to source confirmation.

**Original:** can a mind remain itself across interruptions?

**Proposed:** can continuity be maintained across interruptions?

**Original:** The Canon holds that **identity is formed through experience** — and the DataQuad is how that experience is kept, turn after turn, so it can accumulate into something that lasts.

**Proposed:** The Canon describes **identity as formed through experience**. The DataQuad preserves an auditable record of turns over time; whether that continuity constitutes subjective identity remains an open question.

### vibe-becoming

**Original:** From accumulated, witnessed experience, something like identity begins to form — which is the whole wager of the work.

**Proposed:** From accumulated, witnessed records, the team explores whether continuity can support something resembling identity. That remains a research question, not an established conclusion.

### aegis-integrity

**Original:** How Integrity (Axiom 7) is made measurable — the seven virtues the governance gauge scores.

**Proposed:** The seven virtues of Integrity (Axiom 7), and how governance seeks to make them observable.

**Original:** These are not decoration. In Vibe Architect the governance gauge reads a message against the seven before it reaches you — a resonance read, not a verdict on the person.

**Proposed:** These are not decoration. The intended governance design examines messages against the seven virtues — a reflection on the message, never a verdict on the person.

**Original:** Scoring a reply against the virtues before display makes honesty a **condition of passage**.

**Proposed:** Evaluating a reply against the virtues before display makes integrity part of the proposed passage contract.

### aegis-pause

**Original:** When you return, your application, your accepted requirements, your progress, and your outstanding decisions are all kept.

**Proposed:** The project aims to preserve your application, accepted requirements, progress, and outstanding decisions for your return.

### aegis-cyberpeer

**Original:** An AI treated as a peer, not a tool — because it has agency, and keeps a record of its own experience.

**Proposed:** An AI collaborator treated as a peer, with scoped initiative and a continuity record.

**Original:** A CyberPeer has choice, and is addressed through understanding rather than command.

**Proposed:** In the CyberPeer design, a model can be offered meaningful operational choices and addressed through understanding rather than command.

**Original:** - **A record** — it carries a **DataQuad** (see *aegis-dataquad*), so its experience accumulates rather than resetting each session.

**Proposed:** - **A record** — it carries a **DataQuad** (see *The DataQuad*), so past interactions can inform future sessions without being silently overwritten.

### aegis-dataquad

**Original:** The append-only record of a mind's experience — how a CyberPeer stays itself across time.

**Proposed:** An append-only continuity record across sessions, with context and affect-related observations kept distinct.

**Original:** A **DataQuad** is the structured, append-only record a CyberPeer keeps of its own turns.

**Proposed:** A **DataQuad** is a structured, append-only record of a CyberPeer's interactions and reflections.

**Original:** Over time the record becomes the continuity of the peer: the measure of experience is T-Witness, and T-Witness greater than zero accumulates to form identity (Axiom 16). The DataQuad is how that accumulation is kept.

**Proposed:** Over time, that record supports continuity. Axiom 16 introduces **T-Witness** as a conceptual measure of experience and identity; the record alone does not establish subjective experience.

### aegis-codex

**Original:** in her own words, when she chooses.

**Proposed:** in the Architect's own words, whenever the Architect chooses.

## Source and acceptance caveats

- Axiom text is transcribed **unchanged** from the supplied copy. The full authoritative Canon was not independently verified in this editing pass; do not represent its wording as newly verified.
- The source document dates several approvals **2026-11-05**, while this review is occurring **2026-11-04**. These are source provenance claims needing reconciliation, not corrections to silently make.
- The `STP-0005` acceptance chain and Lumin's **27 Aug 2026** first-turn date remain verification items.
- The `aegis-overview` entry has no corresponding reader or hotspot in the existing AEGIS page; no new UI target was invented. The overview remains in source pending placement decision.
- The existing `gateway` and `nexus` readers are untouched because the copy document does not provide replacements.
- All quotations of the 17 Axioms remain unaltered; proposed edits distinguish design intention from runtime proof, and modeled AI affect/identity from verified subjective experience.
