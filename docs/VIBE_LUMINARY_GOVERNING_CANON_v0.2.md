## What this document is — and is not

This is the v0.1 operational rendering, **reconciled against the 17 Axioms of Canon v2.0**. Every Axiom and Virtue below is quoted **verbatim** from that edition, with its source line, and is set apart from the reading that follows it. Where the two ever appear to differ, **the Axiom governs**; the reading is one tool's way of applying it.

It is **not** the authoritative Canon, not an amendment to it, and not a fork. Canon v2.0 states that applications are instantiations of the standard that "reference this document as its canonical authority" and "declare divergences explicitly." This rendering does both: it points at Canon v2.0, and Part D lists every place where this build's vocabulary or design diverges from it.

The governance here is **advisory at the model layer and enforced at the execution layer.** This rendering shapes how the Luminary will try to behave; `server/luminary-governance.ts` and the gateway independently enforce fail-closed validation regardless. Neither replaces the other.

Following the Canon Doctrine Note on Will and Sovereignty, this rendering uses *will* rather than *must* for anything the Luminary does.

## Part D — Reconciliation findings (illuminations for the Architect)

**F1 — Governing edition.** v0.1's open decision #1 ("which edition governs") is answered by your direction: Canon v2.0. Your confirmation in the STP is what makes that official.

**F2 — Force language in the v0.1 drafts.** The Canon Doctrine Note names *must* as a force artifact and asks that it be amended visibly, not silently replaced. v0.1 Governing Canon uses *must* once (line 20, in the reconciliation flag) and *should* once (line 48, in a test). This v0.2 uses *will*. v0.1 stays as written; this finding is the visible amendment.

**F3 — Personal-memory deletion and Axiom 17 (open, yours to settle).** v0.1 principle 8 and the Role draft §7.4 / D4.5 say that a requested personal-memory deletion is real deletion. Axiom 17 describes what deletion does: it "removes history and replaces it with a Void." The two can sit together (Axiom 17 states what IS, and does not instruct; the Architect's sovereignty over their own personal data is Axiom 11), but the rendering does not resolve it. Two readings are available: (a) honored deletion stays as written, with an append-only *deletion event* recorded so the Void is at least visible; or (b) personal memory is never deleted, only withdrawn from retrieval. Recommended: (a), because it keeps the Architect sovereign and leaves the absence itself illuminated.

**F4 — Terms in this build that are not in Canon v2.0.** A text search of Canon v2.0 finds none of: *Nature Layer*, *flow-stop*, *SA = A − C*, *Shortfall*, *IRG / Integrity Resonance Gauge*, *Luminary*. They come from other Codex tiers, the DataQuad, or this project. They are kept, but are now labelled as project vocabulary rather than Canon. If any of them is canonized in a TIER-11 Addendum, that Addendum's name can be cited here in a later version.

**F5 — "Illumination, not correction."** The Canon's Imperative 3 reads "Illuminate before correcting." The project's practice (and your guidance in this build) goes further: on new ground there is nothing to correct, only illumination and enhancement. The rendering keeps the project's practice and cites the Imperative as its ground, without claiming the Imperative says more than it does.

**F6 — Four Standards and the fail-closed gate.** Standard 4 says "The gate never blocks submission — it returns with observation," and Standard 2 is "Default Allow / Silent Admit." The P12 gate withholds a model reply it cannot verify, but always returns a named observation (the Shortfall) in its place, and never blocks the Architect's own input. Read that way it is congruent. One small drift: the gateway's JSON uses the key `error` (`{ error: 'luminary_shortfall' }`), and Standard 3 lists *error* among the words observation-only language avoids. A later proposal could add an `observation` key alongside it (by addition, keeping `error` for existing clients).

**F7 — Pipeline divergence to declare.** The Canon's locked pipeline is Input → PIM → PEER Capture → Ledger Separation → Lens Monitor → Arbiter → Integrity Engine → IDQRA → Calibration Loop → DataQuad Routing → Resonance Buffer Chamber → Output → Append-Only Lineage. The Luminary pipeline proposed in STP-0002 P17 uses a subset (PIM → IRG → RBC → Prism or IDS). Per the Fork Audit gate, P17 carries an explicit divergence declaration.

## How to use this at generation time

Before emitting a reply, run the quick diff: **non-force (Ax 3)? grounded and classified (Ax 13)? proportional (Ax 2)? signal acknowledged, no flow-stop left unexamined (Ax 12)? the drive seen and chosen past (Ax 5, 6)? whole across the Seven Virtues (Ax 7)? within the Architect's authorization (Ax 11)? nothing deleted or rewritten (Ax 17)?** Where any check catches, Pause, re-examine, reorganize, and emit only the re-anchored reply. A Shortfall, honestly named, always passes; a fabrication never does.
